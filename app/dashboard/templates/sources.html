Discovery sources

Greenhouse and Lever both run free, public, unauthenticated job-board APIs meant for exactly this kind of use -- not every company has a board on either, and the token/slug isn't always guessable from the company name. Verify one before adding it: curl https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/<token>/jobs or curl "https://api.lever.co/v0/postings/<company>?mode=json" -- a 200 with real jobs means it's valid, a 404 means it isn't on that platform.

Verified: "inmobi" has InMobi's real, current openings.

What's not included, and why

Company career pages beyond Greenhouse/Lever aren't supported generically -- each one has its own markup and terms, so a generic scraper would be unreliable at best and could violate a specific site's terms at worst. If you find a company with its own public, documented job-listing API, tell me and I'll build a dedicated adapter for it the same way Greenhouse and Lever's were built: verified against a real response before being trusted.
